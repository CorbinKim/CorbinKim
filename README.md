### 🧭 Research Focus

I research intelligent controller design for Open RAN, with a focus on building programmable and adaptable wireless networks.

My work is part of the **FALCON project**, where I explore the development of **xApps, rApps, and dApps**, as well as the integration of **digital twins** for UAV-assisted wireless connectivity.

- 📡 **Open RAN and RAN Intelligent Controllers (RICs)**
